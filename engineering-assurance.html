Engineering & Assurance

Translate approved designs into executable requirements, engineering controls and verifiable outcomes.

Discuss engineering and assurance

What problem can you solve for me?

The design is agreed. Then delivery changes it, and nobody can show where.

  • Specifications that cannot be tested against what was built.
  • Decisions that drift between the design and the running service.
  • Evidence assembled after the event, for an audit.
  • People, services and agents acting with no proof that each stayed within its authority.

What will I receive?

What was agreed is what runs, with the evidence to show it.

  • An architecture and an executable specification.
  • Conformance tests that run against the implementation.
  • An evidence design: what the service records, and how it is examined.

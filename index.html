The consequence

We are fixing AI rather than redesigning the organisation it has exposed.

When execution becomes cheap and fast, the constraint moves to deciding, authorising, coordinating and proving.

Who can act?

Capability is not authority.

Who decides?

Decisions, not defaults.

Who is accountable?

A named person, not a system.

Can you prove it?

Evidence, not assertion.

Agents can act in seconds while authority, accountability and evidence still span documents, roles and systems designed for human-speed organisations.

▼

The proposition

Don't redesign the organisation around agents. Design it around the work.

The job should not depend on whether the work is performed by a person, an AI agent, a service or software. The organisation should know what job is being done, who or what is performing it, under whose authority, for what value, and with what evidence.

{% include diagrams/job-participant.html %}

The job is invariant. The participant is a governed selection: it still decides skills, controls, risk, cost and accountability.

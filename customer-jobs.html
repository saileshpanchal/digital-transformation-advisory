Turn customer needs into working, testable propositions with accountability and protection built in.

Discuss a proposition

What problem can you solve for me?

The proposition is designed first, and the permissions, protections and controls arrive later.

  • Journeys are designed apart from the organisation that has to run them.
  • Family members, helpers and agents act for customers with no designed authority.
  • Controls are added after the design, and change the experience.
  • Nobody can operate the proposition until most of the money is spent.

What will I receive?

A proposition specified to the point of an investment decision, with the customer in control by design.

  • The customer job, and the journey that serves it.
  • The participant and authority design: who may do what, what the customer authorises, and what the institution checks.
  • A journey specification with its acceptance criteria.
  • A delivery approach, and what you need for the investment decision.

How the work runs

  • Finds the job the customer needs done, in their own words.
  • Designs the journey with its participants, permissions and protections built in, and the decisions the institution keeps.
  • Writes the design as a specification that can be tested.
  • Builds one agreed journey as a working proof, where you choose the 90-day Proof.

What we do

DTA doesn't simply advise organisations on transformation.

It helps them understand how they work, design what needs to change and demonstrate that their decisions can be implemented and evidenced.

What do you need to achieve?

Four offers. Each one says the problem it solves, what you receive, and where you can see the approach working.

Make better decisions

Strategic Advisory

Make better investment, transformation and technology decisions, independently challenged and grounded in evidence.

The problem
A decision is in front of you, and it needs independent challenge before money commits.
What you receive: a decision
A written position on the decision: the options considered, the evidence behind the recommendation, and what was ruled out.
Why believe it
Advice is given to one executive on one decision, so there is no working example to run here. What you can check is who advises you, and what DTA has published. About DTA

Understand your organisation

Organisational Discovery & Design

Discover how your organisation really works, where value is lost and what needs to change.

The problem
Nobody can show how the organisation actually works.
What you receive: an organisational understanding
An evidenced model of how your organisation works: its jobs, decisions, authorities and dependencies, each traced to its source, with a recommended redesign.
See it working

How an organisation's jobs, work and accountability can be seen as one model. Select any element and switch between its views: what customers need, the work that creates, and who performs it.

Design customer propositions

Customer Jobs & Proposition Design

Turn customer needs into working, testable propositions with accountability and protection built in.

The problem
The proposition is designed first, and the permissions, protections and controls arrive later.
What you receive: a proposition design
The customer job, the participant and authority design, a journey specification and acceptance criteria, ready for an investment decision. A working proof is a separate, optional engagement.
See it working
Set up a regular bill Working experience on the live bank
How a customer need becomes a working proposition. Evelyn wants help with a bill. A helper prepares it, Evelyn decides, and the bank checks. The money is synthetic.

Engineer and assure change

Engineering & Assurance

Translate approved designs into executable requirements, engineering controls and verifiable outcomes.

The problem
The design is agreed. Then delivery changes it, and nobody can show where.
What you receive: an assurance
An executable specification, conformance tests that run against the implementation, and a design for the evidence the service records.
See it working
The same journey, open to inspection Working experience on the live bank
How the same proposition's decisions, permissions, controls and evidence can be inspected and tested. Run the journey, then open any step under Behind the bank: who acted, the checks the bank made and the facts it recorded. Behind the bank has no address of its own, so this opens the journey at its start.

What problem can you solve for me?

Nobody can show how the organisation actually works.

  • Decisions were made and never recorded, so nobody can say why things are as they are.
  • It is unclear who may decide, approve or act, and people work around the gap.
  • Knowledge leaves when people do.
  • Change is planned against an organisation chart, not against the work.

What will I receive?

You can see your organisation as it works: its jobs, who decides, and what a change would move.

  • Findings you can check: each decision, authority and gap, with where it came from.
  • A model of the organisation's jobs, work and dependencies.
  • A recommended redesign, and what it would move.

How the work runs

  • Investigates one bounded part of the organisation from the evidence it already has: documents, systems and the people who do the work.
  • Recovers the decisions, authorities, dependencies and value behind that work, each traced to its source.
  • Builds a connected model of the jobs, the work and who performs it.
  • Designs the change with its consequences in view, before anyone commits to it.

MTDR provides an open way to record organisational decisions and their supporting context. DTA's discovery approach uses it to make that knowledge visible, connected and usable.
